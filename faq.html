FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What age can a student start music lessons?

Students can begin music education at different ages depending on the instrument and programme. Contact us to discuss the most suitable starting point.

Do I need previous musical experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Our Initial Grading Programme is designed to establish the fundamentals required for musical development.

What instruments do you teach?

Piano, Violin, Viola, Cello, Flute, Acoustic Guitar, Electric Guitar, Bass Guitar, Drums and Vocals.

How often are lessons, and how long?

Lessons are held once per week, structured around a one-hour weekly session.

How many students are in a class?

Initial Grading and Standard Certification programmes have a maximum of 3 students per class. Premium Certification is one-on-one.

Do you prepare students for ABRSM?

Yes. Our Standard and Premium Certification programmes provide structured preparation toward the ABRSM examination pathway.

Can adults join?

Yes. Music education is not limited to children — adults interested in developing their musical abilities are welcome to enquire.

Can students perform at concerts and events?

Yes. Students may have opportunities to participate in concerts, showcases, competitions and other performances depending on their level and readiness.

Do you provide instruments?

Instrument availability varies. Contact the Centre for information about available instruments and equipment.

Can I hire Robin's musicians for my event?

Yes. We provide professional performance services, including solo musicians, Cuatro Strings and District 7.

How do I register?

Complete the online application form and our team will contact you regarding placement, scheduling and next steps.
